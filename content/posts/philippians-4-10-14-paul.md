#### MISUSE
If you've been to church any length of time you probably heard someone quoting it. Don't you? Personally I've heard it many times. And unfortunately not always people truly understand what it actually says. And simply give it their own meaning. Which fits whatever circumstances or the context they happen to be in.

Unfortunately miss interpretation of various parts of the scriptures becomes more and more common. Biblical truths are bent to align with the contemporary view of the world. Instead of figuring out what bible actually say, we assume the meaning of our own. We no longer trying to understand the authors intent, but instead replace it with our own idea of what this text mean.

Sometimes it intentional, but more often it is because we are not taught how to study the bible!

This forces us to default to the standards way of studying written texts. The ways we used to study literature in school and university.  We read the scripture and ask ourselves:

“How do **I** feel about this text?”
“What does it mean to **me**?”
“How do **I** understand this?”

And if you recognize yourself. I don't blame you. It's not your fault.
…

“You are holding it wrong bro!” / “You doing it wrong“

…

You might not like what I'm saying or disagree. As I did when I first came across this issue. But, we should change our approach to the bible study. Instead of focusing on ourselves. Instead of asking what does it mean to **ME**. We should start asking other kinds of questions:

“What is the author is saying?”
“What was the author’s intent?”

This is what important! By divine revelation author of the book wrote it. And we need to figure out what was God's idea when he put together this text. It's not us who give the meaning to the text. It is the Lord. Our role is to figure out His idea and how it apples to us.

When the text were only written it was much more simple. People understood perfectly what the text mean. It was written in the context of their time, language and culture. But, as more time passes more careful we should be about the assumptions we make.
