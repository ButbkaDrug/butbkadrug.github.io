### BORN AGAIN
Two weeks ago we talked about **stripping off the "old self"**. That coming to faith is not about **ADDING** something to your life. We saw that Bible describing coming to faith as something very dramatic. Like being born again. Starting from scratch. New beginning where you leave your OLD life as if you DIED for this world, and you starting a new life in Christ.

### NEW CREATION
We read that in the kingdom of God we become new creation. We put on a **NEW SELF**. That has amazing characteristic. It's being continuously renewed. Also, as a **new creation** we get NEW IDENTITY as children of God. Which makes the differences that we have in this world irrelevant.


### Everything Changes
When you come to Christ you become a **new person**. Being born again changes everything. We put on a new self. Which is **being renewed** continuously. I feel like we should spend a minute thinking about  **renewal**.

About the process that begins in us when we get born again. About the changes that accrue inside of us. When we accept the new identity of children of God.

It's a process. When we undergo some transformations. The way we think, our attitudes, our goals and values. The invisible side of us starts to change. Then those changes which no one can see, start to spill out, start to render out into visible areas of our lives. In the ways we behave. In the ways we talk and treat others.

So, it's very important to understand the nature of those changes. Visible change is the result of the changed hard. And not just showing expected behavior.

### Circus
Historically, circus trainers used a combination of fear-based conditioning, punishment, coercion, and food rewards to make the animals perform the tricks. 

Relying on the method of **aversive conditioning**: an animal learns to avoid an unpleasant experience by performing the demanded behaviour.

The important caveat in all this: is that even though that tiger performing a trick, it doesn't necessarily mean it has been domesticated. It remains a wild animal whose behaviour has been conditioned in captivity.

In other words tiger didn't change. It just learned to behave a certain way when needed.

...

### NOT THE WAY
I bring this example, because sometimes we fall into a trap of think that it is the same in the kingdom of God. That Christianity is about proper conduct. And our task is to make people to behave properly. And that's it. Then individuals and churches start to use same methods as animal trainers to achieve that goal.

Which is not the way of the kingdom.

### Hypocrites 
We are not called to go into the world and make hypocrite. Who behave in public, but go unhinged when no one watching.  Pharisees in the day of Jesus were often like that. And oh boy, Jesus have had things to tell them...

So, enforcing right behaviors is not the way. It does not bring changes. It is not a renewal that we read about.

### Can't sit around ether
But on the flip side one cannot just sit around waiting for the changes to come. For bad habits and attitudes to magically disappear. Because, often they won't. 

So, what do we do?

### VERBS
If we were to re-read all the verses in the chapter 3. We would see that they full of verbs like: "PUT ON\OFF", "LET", "DO THIS" or "STOP DOING THAT"...

That's the beginning of the answer. Even though God produces His work in us. He is not going to forcefully change you. He doesn't take over our will, making you to do the right thing because he knows better.

That's not how the loving Father works. He enables us to make the right choice instead!

God gives us a chance to chose HIM. Even though He is almighty and all powerful. And we simply cannot resist His will if He wants it to be so. But, yet GOD chooses not to force us into things. But he let's us to exercise our will. 

**He want's you to chose Him and His ways**

Sure thing He helps us. Especially with impossible, or very hard things like addictions and stuff. He does real miracles. I myself experienced it first hand. He revealed Himself to us. He revealed his will. He told us exactly what to do and how to live. He asked us: "Please, choice LIFE!". But He leaves the choice to us.

...

Renewal that we read about has nothing to do with fear-based conditioning, punishment and coercion. Instead it's based on love. And the freedom of choose.

So, when we read verbs in our bibles. It's not about earning salvation or God's favor... You can't do anything to earn salvation. And you can't do anything to make God to love you more then he already loves you.

It's only shows us the ways how we can choose HIM.

...

Keeping up with a new life is about **allowing** renewal to happen. It's about willingly **accepting** the changes God wants to produce in us. And work together with him.

### MOTIVATION
Which brings us to the motivation and driving force behind all that:


### Colossians 3:17
> ¹⁷Whatever you do in word or deed, *do* everything in the name of the Lord Jesus, giving thanks through Him to God the Father.

I think we can spend a long time talking about this verse. Because, it's so good! I find it's so relieving and empowering.

It gives strength to do the hard things, that otherwise one would probably not do.

For example to forgive a difficult person. I forgive not because the other person deserves it. Or realized how wrong he\she were. Or even admits his fault. None of that matters. I can forgive simply in the name of Jesus. For God's glory.

You can decide to help someone. Even though it will costs you something. Not necessarily because you such a good person. Maybe you are. I know I'm not. But you still can do it in the name of Jesus, for the Glory of God.

It does help me personally to do the things I dislike very much. Hard things that I don't enjoy doing. And frankly would not do for any other reason. I say to myself "As much as I hate it. I'll do it in the name of Jesus. For the Glory of God".

It can help us to deal with people, relationships and the challenges of life. But also sometimes we have to utilize this approach when we chose to submit to the word of God that might be more challenging to agree with. Or we not yet fully understand.

> ¹⁸Wives, be subject to your husbands, as is fitting in the Lord.¹⁹Husbands, love your wives and do not become bitter against them.²⁰Children, obey your parents in everything, for this is pleasing to the Lord.²¹Fathers, do not antagonize your children, so that they will not become discouraged.

As we read this verses you might feel personally attacked. You might dislike what you read. Or maybe you don't care, I don't know... Regardless of your stands let's try to stay objective and see what the bible actually says:

In this text we read about one the most fundamental units of the society. The family. We'll be looking at 4 types of relationships that potentially needs to undergo transformation(actually more but we stop here for now): 

* Wives to husbands
* Husbands to wives
* Children to parents
* Fathers* to children

This entire chapter talks about renewal and transition from worldly to godly. And this section if one of the most important ones. Especially when we will be looking at the changes that has to accrue in Husband to Wife relationship. Because in the context of the first century, very little had to change for wives and a lot for Husbands.

But let's start in order in verse 18:

### Colossians 3:18
> ¹⁸Wives, be subject(*submit to*) to your husbands, as is fitting in the Lord.

Let's break this one down first.

### Misconception 
Very first thing that drew my attention regarding this verse is that a lot of guys are getting very exited about it... I honestly can't really think of the reason why?

But because it's a common misconception in certain christian circles I would have to mention that:

Bible does not say: "Husbands, make sure your wife submits to you."

This verse also do not provide the basis to demand submission, like you know: "Bible says you should submit to me... Because I'm the man...". No. Wrong.

As we explored earlier this whole chapter is about **WILLINGLY EXCEPTING** Godly order or things. It's about allowing God to change us. For his glory and for our benefit as well.

With that said. Let's see what it actually say:

### Subject
The verb that tends to catch everybody's attention is **subject**/**submit**. Which was the military term that meant *to place oneself under authority* **voluntary**.

I was looking for good definition that will be easy to understand, but will still preserve the original meaning as much as possible and that's what I found:

*To willingly accept your place and responsibilities in a relationship, rather than fighting for control*

In military world submission can be harsh and brutal. Submission can be demanded and demanding. But in the kingdom of God everything is done differently. Through love. And love does not demand, it seeking to give rather then receive.

So, what this section is encouraging - it is to **willingly accept** to submit. 

Which doesn't mean you cannot have an opinion. I doesn't mean you cannot disagree. It doesn't mean you have less of a value or that you are less important. None of that. It is mostly about letting go of the control.

Which is something that is hard to do. It is less scary when you husband is devoted follower of Christ just like you. And trying his best to adhere to biblical standards as much as possible. Then you are in the safe place, because bible says:

### Ephesians 5:25
> ²⁵Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ also loved the church and gave Himself up for her...

It's a pretty hight standard.

But what if he is NOT a christian?

### Believer husband vs unbeliever husband
Which brings us to the important point. I think that this situation, when a husband is not a believer,  can be one of the main reason for this text:

Usually when we read this verses we think of the modern day family, probably our own or our parents or people we know well. People meet, liked each other, fall in love and get married.

But, back in the day things were different. A lot of marriages were arranged marriages. Sometimes falling in love was a part of the deal. But, other times, quite often it wasn't. You've got what you've got and now you have to live with it. Wife inevitably were under her husbands authority as it was a social reality of the day. Regardless whether a husband good or bad she had to submit to him. It was not optional. She could not divorce or leave. She were trapped.

What happens when she comes to Christ? Maybe both of them believed and then it's easier. But more likely as it often happens a women believes first. She get's born again. She put's on a NEW SELF. And now what?

**What do I do now with that ungodly man I have to obey?**

*To willingly choose to submit to him*

Not because you don't have another choice. This time you can do it in the name of Jesus. For the Glory of God.

This can yield some amazing results:

### 1 Peter 3:1,2
> ..wives, be subject to your own husbands so that even if any *of them* are disobedient to the word, they may be won over without a word by the behavior of their wives,²as they observe your pure and respectful behavior.

You can win your husbands heart! And then the common "**I'll change him**" mentality will come true!

...

### Design but no implementation.
This scriptures describe Godly design. The attitudes we should have. The invisible side of things. But this verses say very little about concrete implementation as a programmer would say. They don't talk about who should do what in the family.

Because, that's depends on two of you. Bible lives the room for a diversity here. As there is no two identical marriages, there is no reasons to assume that all the households should be identical.

### LIMITS
We also should talk about some limitations contained in this verse. Back in the day husbands had a lot of authority over their wives in legal and in all other senses. Sometimes their authority was almost unlimited.

And word of God places some limits to that authority: 

### Colossians 3:18
> ¹⁸...to your(own) husbands...

### To your own husband
Fist limit is **to your own husband**. I don't think we have problems of such sort in our fellowship or society. But you never know. We did have some strangers who made wired claims that according to the bible any woman should submit to any man. But it's not the true at all. And this verse(and not only this verse) debunks that lie.

Do not forget that we only talk about family relationship. Relationships between husband and wife. Not generic relationship between man and women.

Second, this verse very clearly specifies that wives it to subjects to her own husband and not someone else. 

It feels wired having to say that. But you gotta do, what you gotta do.

### As it is fitting in the Lord

### Colossians 3:18
> ¹⁸...as is fitting in the Lord.

The other limit is "as it is fitting in the Lord". Which means that BIBLE still holds supreme authority. And you are free not to do ungodly things, Even if your husband insists on it. Which becomes increasingly important when your husband doesn't share your faith in God.

When I was a teenager. My mom was a believer, but my dad wasn't. Everything was complicated in our family in general. In today's terms they would call it "dysfunctional family". But, back in a day it was considered "a family"... Anyway. At some point even though my parents didn't planed it, but my mom got pregnant...

My dad was alcoholic, with some issues, so he didn't want another kid to take care of. He insisted on an abortion.Like, for real. He were trying to put his food down on it.

And what do? Should you submit to your husband as a good christian?

Of course not! That's exactly the king of situation when submitting was "not fitting" in the Lord.

She didn't do it. Even though she had to pay the price for it and suffer a whole bunch.
