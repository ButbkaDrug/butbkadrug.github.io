Let's break down this verses and see what they say. Starting in verse 10:

#### Philippians 4:10
 ¹⁰But I rejoiced in the Lord greatly, that now at last you have revived your concern for me; indeed, you were concerned *before,* but you lacked an opportunity *to act.*

### CONTEXT
In order to truly understand this passage, we need a little bit of a context. If you've been following along throughout the whole book, you already know all this. But for those who are new here, I'll sprinkle in a little bit of an additional information that will help us to correctly perceive what Bible is saying here.

This letter is written by apostle Paul. One of the greatest Apostles of the New Testament. Who started a lot of churches. Who were main advocate of "Salvation by faith, for non Jews!".

He wrote this letter to the church of the city of Philippi, to the brothers and sisters who were concerned about his situation. Because, at the time of writing this Paul is in the Roman prison for his faith in Christ.

He writes a letter to his friends, the Church that he started a number of years ago. Church that has been his partner in ministry for many years. Doing the same work of the gospel. Sharing the same struggles. But, also supporting Paul financially. Enabling Paul to continue Lord's work. They did this for years.

But then something happened. Church didn't support Paul for a while. Maybe it was due to the Paul's circumstances. Because he was all over the place, while being escorted into the Roman prison going.  Undergoing the most crazy obstacles. Or maybe it was something on the other end. Maybe something were going on in Philippi. Most likely a little bit of both.

But in the end, they figured it all out. And got it going again. They sent one of their members to take care of Paul's needs while he is under arrest. And also they send enough money to make sure Paul is well supplied.

Remember Paul is under house arrest. He is unable to work and earn money. He doesn't have passive income. Or savings to live off. He is not even unable to go anywhere. But He still have to pay rent. And we all know how much of a burden that might be. Especially when you don't have a job that pays. 

Also someone needs to do the shopping. Groceries, toilet paper idk. you name it. Some basic stuff. 

With that said let's go back to our text: 

#### Philippians 4:10
> ¹⁰But I rejoiced in the Lord greatly, that now at last you have revived your concern for me; indeed, you were concerned *before,* but you lacked an opportunity *to act.*

In other words Paul is saying:

> I'm so happy you care for me again.

The wording of the original text is quite poetic. And bears the imagery of the plants that re-sprout from the ground again in the spring.

Paul saying that he is very happy. Because the church renewed their commitment to support him. As soon as they've had an opportunity. 

Important note here is that Paul's reasons for joy might not be what you think it is. He says: "*I rejoice in the Lord*". We gotta talk about the concept of **IN THE LORD** one day, in  more details. But as for today.

The reason for Paul's joy is not the help he is receiving. Which would be my immediate though. Because he were in the very tight situation in many ways for quite a time. So, it is only make sense to be happy this period is over. And at least you don't have to worry about financial side of things anymore.

No, it's not that. It's not about Paul at all. The reason for joy is that the church have dedicated to live, to act, to be obedient to the Lord's way of doing things.

### IN THE LORD IN A NUTSHELL
This is what **IN THE LORD** means in a nutshell.

There is a Worldy way of doing things.  Like in the time of Paul there were Roman way of doing things. In our reality there is a Georgian way of doing things - the patterns, norms and expectations dictated by society, culture and history. Something that considered "normal" and "expected" in particular time and place. 

### EXAMPLE ###

And there is alternative way. The way we called to live as citizens of heaven. The Lord's way of doing things. And when we choose to live our life the Lord's way we choose the right thing. It brings us a lot of good in a long run. Even though it might not be the easiest choice in the moment.

So, in case of Philippians, Paul rejoices about them. And not about himself. Confirmation of that we read in the following verses:

### Philippians 4:11,12
> ¹¹Not that I speak from need, for I have learned to be content in whatever *circumstances* I am.¹²I know how to get along with little, and I also know how to live in prosperity; in any and every *circumstance* I have learned the secret of being filled and going hungry, both of having abundance and suffering need.

This verses are packed with important and essential truth.

### FIRST 
On the very surface we see Paul saying that he is not after riches. On one hand he says "I'm so happy to receive your gift." But on the other hand he makes a strong point that it's not about him, or his financial situation.

His rejoices because he can see the fruit of their faith. Their maturity. That they still live it the Lord's way.

Later in verse 17, after affirmation of their support of his ministry in verses 15 and 16. Paul says:

### Philippians 4:17
> ¹⁷I don’t say this because I want a gift from you. Rather, I want you to receive a reward for your kindness.

Indicating that by supporting Paul and\in his ministry, people did well. You never lose by partaking in mission. By supporting those who doing the Lord's work. I think it is the best investment you can possible make. And also, it is the Lord's will for us to do so.
