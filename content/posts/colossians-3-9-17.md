Greetings dear friends, we continue to work our way through the book of Colossians. Currently we are going though the chapter 3. Hopefully today we will cover verses from 9 until 17.

Generally speaking chapter 3 talk's about transformation. Verses 1-4 talk about old self. Describes life of so called "song of disobedience". Or simply put worldly lifestyle.

Old self has to be thrown away, it is no good for a new life with God. That's why this whole process of a genuine repentance described in a bible as death for this world,  and a second birth for a new life in Christ...

I find this to be a very dramatic image. Very radical example. And again, just to be clear. Espesially for those who are new to church. Bible talk figuratively, it uses the images of the things we know, understand and have seen, to describe spiritual reality that is hidden from us.

So, repentance becomes the beginning of a very dramatic change, of a great transformation.

Which means that you cannot continue to live the way this world lives. Those are mutually exclusive.

### Example
Imagine a person. There are actually ton of them out there. You might be one of them. Who dream about transforming his body. You know grow some muscles, drop a little weight, improve mobility. And this person have genuine desire. He watches fitness videos every day. Digs into the science of bodybuilding and fat loss. Slowly becoming an expert in this areas.

But as long as this person continue to live unhealthy lifestyle. He's condition will remain the same. Muscles do not grow from watching YouTube. Weight does not go down until you change your habits. Mobility not gonna improve all by itself. In order to see some radical improvement, you have to radically change your lifestyle.

### Back to kingdom

### STRIP OFF
With that said, I want to bring your attention to the end of the verse 9 and beginning of the verse 10 where it says:

### Colossians 3:9(NASB2020)
> ⁹...you stripped off the old self/man with its *evil* practices, ¹⁰and have put on the new *self* which is being renewed...

Once again it refers to what we've said in the beginning. The change that accrue when you turn to God. 

In this verses we can see two different, I would even say apposing instances of "self". **OLD** self and **NEW** self. OLD has to be stripped off, and NEW needs to be put on. Each one of them have it's core component highlighted for us.

### OLD SELF
Old self - comes with it's "practices" - [parxis] in Greek. Which is a *deliberate action. Practical application of a theory, belief or knowledge*. Which brings us to the last week's topic. When we  looked at the "practices" of the old self. Deliberate practices. Actions that are exercised based on once's knowledge, beliefs or theory that they have.

### NEW SELF
On the other hand there is a "NEW" self. New identity as a child of God:

### Colossians 3:10
¹⁰...new *self* which is being renewed to a true knowledge according to the image of the One who created it —

We see the **NEW SELF** and that it is being "renewed". Which is not the same as **restored**!

Hear me out, it is not about restoring some previous state. Like a car restoration. When the take old broken car and restore it to it's original factory state. 

No, renewal is something a bit different. This **new self** get's characteristics and abilities **old self** never have had.

### Genuine repentance 
When you come to faith. When you repent and turn to God it changes everything. It's not just yet another thing that's get added to our life. Like another hobby you picked up. Or one more club that you joined. If you Christianity looks like that, you are not there yet. That's not the faith in Christ described in the bible.

Biblical idea of faith in Christ, or Kingdom of God. Is way more radical. It is compared to dying and being born again. Or like we read today becoming completely NEW person. Our life splits in two chapters "before" Christ and "since" Christ. 

Being christian cannot be just some part of your identity. No, it should becomes a core of it.

### MOST IMPORTANT
This is probably one single most important event in your entire life. When we look at all the images that describe Kingdom of God. Describe encountering of Christ. Coming to faith. All the parables that Jesus used to teach people. They all carry the same image. It is about giving up the old and getting something completely new. It is something so precious, that it is totally worth giving everything up for.

It is something that get's added to our life, but something that completely overwrites your life.

### NOT a fan club
All it is to say that Follower of Christ is not just a fan club, not a hangout that on Sunday nights. It's a complete transformation. It is like a new birth. Becoming a follower of Christ is  about becoming a new person.

> **It is impossible to continue to live the life that brought you to the broken state and anticipate the change.**

### Colossians 3:11
> ¹¹*a renewal* in which there is no *distinction between* Greek and Jew, circumcised and uncircumcised, barbarian, Scythian, slave, *and* free, but Christ is all, and in all.

In this verse we can see that the change is not limited by behavior. Kingdom of God redefines society. Illuminating social ladders. Making salvation, God's love and acceptance available to everyone with no distinction. In the Kingdom of God your background does not matter:

    * Greek - top tear. Hight class. Education, culture, philosopy - the best of the world(at the time). Often portrait like new God's people.

    * Jews - originally God's people. Original recepients of the promises. 

    * Circumsized - part of the old covenent

    * Unceircusized - were viewed as outsiders.

    * Barbarians - foreigners who would not speak Greak. 

    * Scythion - completely savage, uncivilized and wild nation that lived North of the Black sea.

    * Slave - wos not even considered real people. More like property

    * Freeman - regular everyday person.

Often each of this was the point of tension and separation, division. Greeks vs Jews, Circumsized vs Uncircumsized. Outsiders like Barbarians, Scythian and slaves. But in the God's kingdom there is no place for this separation. We all became new people. No matter where we come from. We all got new identity. Verse 12:

### Colossians 3:12
> ¹²...those who have been chosen of God, holy and beloved...

When we come to Christ. When we become citizens of His kingdom. We should go all in. Strip off the OLD SELF and put on a NEW SELF. Accept the new way of life. New mentality. New order of thing.
