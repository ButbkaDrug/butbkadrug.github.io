Generally speaking chapter 3 talk's about transformation. Verses 1-4 talk about old self. Describes life of so called "song of disobedience". Or simply put worldly lifestyle. Lifestyle that opposes God.

### Thrown away
And because it opposes God old self has to be thrown away, for it is no good for a new life with God. That's why this whole process of a genuine repentance described in a bible as death for this world,  and a second birth for a new life in Christ...

### Dramatic image
I find this to be a very dramatic image. Very radical example. And again, just to be clear. Especially for those who are new to church. Bible talk figuratively, it uses the images of the things we know, understand and have seen, to describe spiritual reality that is hidden from us.

So, repentance becomes the beginning of a radical change, of a great transformation. Which means that you cannot continue to live the old way after repentance. Wordily lifestyle and life with God are mutually exclusive.

### Example
Let's imagine a person. You might be one of such people or not. Who dream about transforming his body. You know... Growing some muscle, dropping a little weight, improve mobility...

Let's say this person have genuine desire to change. He watches fitness videos every day. Digs into the science of bodybuilding, flexibility and fat loss. Slowly becoming a home grown expert in this areas.

But as long as this person continue to live unhealthy lifestyle. His condition will remain the same. Muscles not gonna grow from watching YouTube. Weight not gonna go down until you change your habits. Mobility not gonna improve without stretching. 

Unwanted truth is that in order to see some radical improvement, you have to radically change your lifestyle. Right? Sometime your current lifestyle is not compatible with the changes you want to achieve. And there is no way around it.

> **It is impossible to continue to live the life that brought you to the broken state and anticipate the change.**

### STRIP OFF
With that said, I want to draw your attention to the end of the verse 9 and beginning of the verse 10 where it says:

### Colossians 3:9(NASB2020)
> ⁹...you stripped off the old self/man with its *evil* practices, ¹⁰and have put on the new *self* which is being renewed...

Once again it refers to the repentance. Something that I've already mentioned in the beginning. The radical change that accrue when you turn to God. 

In this verses we can see two different, I would even say apposing instances of "self". **OLD** self and **NEW** self. OLD has to be stripped off, and NEW needs to be put on. Each one of them have it's core component highlighted for us.

### OLD SELF
Old self - defined by it's "practices" - [parxis] in Greek. Which are *deliberate actions. Practical application of a theory, belief or knowledge*. That once again reminds us about  last week's topic. When we looked at the "*practices*", **deliberate actions** of the old self. That are exercised based on worldly knowledge, beliefs or theory.

### NEW SELF
On the other hand we have a "NEW" self. New identity in Christ:

### Colossians 3:10
¹⁰...new *self* which is being renewed to a true knowledge according to the image of the One who created it —

**NEW SELF** defined by the process of "renewal". Which is not the same as **restoration**!

I want us to note, that "PUT ON a NEW SELF" is not about returning to some previous state.

It's not like a car restoration. When you take an old broken car and restore it to it's original factory state.

No, renewal is something different. This **new self** get's characteristics and abilities **old self** never have.

It's like sticking the jet engine and adding wings making car able to fly or something. Adding new abilities.

### Genuine repentance 
When you come to faith. When you repent and turn to God it changes everything. It's not just yet another thing that's get added to our life. It is not like yet another hobby you picked up. Or one more club that you joined. If your Christianity looks like that, you are not there yet. That's not the faith in Christ described by the bible.

Biblical idea of faith in Christ, or Kingdom of God. Is way more radical. It is compared to dying and being born again. Or like we read today **becoming completely NEW person**. It's situation when our life splits in two stages "before" Christ and "since" Christ. You should be able to relate to that.

**Being christian** cannot be just some part of our identity, part of our life.  **It should become a our core.**

### MOST IMPORTANT
This is probably one single most important event in your entire life. When we look at all the images that describe Kingdom of God. Describe encounter with Christ. Coming to faith. When we look at all the parables that Jesus used to teach people about the Kingdom of God. They all carry the same image, same idea.

It is about giving up the old life and getting something completely new. It is something so precious, that it is totally worth giving everything up for.

Not just something that get's added to our present life, but something that completely overwrites your life.

### NOT a fan club
All it is to say that Followers of Christ is not just a fan club, not a hangout on Sunday nights. It's a complete transformation. Like a new birth. Becoming a follower of Christ is about becoming a new person.

### Colossians 3:11
> ¹¹*a renewal* in which there is no *distinction between* Greek and Jew, circumcised and uncircumcised, barbarian, Scythian, slave, *and* free, but Christ is all, and in all.

In this verse we can see that the change is not limited by behavior. Kingdom of God redefines every part of life and society. Illuminating social ladders. Making salvation and God's love, His acceptance available to everyone with no distinction. In the Kingdom of God your background does not matter:

    * Greek - top tear. Hight class. Education, culture, philosopy - the best of the world(at the time). Often portrait like new God's people.

    * Jews - originally God's people. Original recepients of the promises. 

    * Circumsized - part of the old covenent

    * Unceircusized - were viewed as outsiders.

    * Barbarians - foreigners who would not speak Greak. 

    * Scythion - completely savage, uncivilized and wild nation that lived North of the Black sea.

    * Slave - wos not even considered real people. More like property

    * Freeman - regular everyday person.

Often each of this was the point of tension and separation, division. Greeks vs Jews, Circumsized vs Uncircumsized. Outsiders like Barbarians, Scythian and slaves. But in the God's kingdom there is no place for this separation. We all became new people. No matter where we come from. We all got new identity. Verse 12:

### Colossians 3:12
> ¹²...those who have been chosen of God, holy and beloved...

When we come to Christ. When we become citizens of His kingdom. We should go all in. Strip off the OLD SELF and put on a NEW SELF. Accept the new way of life. New mentality. New order of thing.
