Greetings church, we continue to work our way through the book of Colossians. Today we going to conclude the chapter 2. We will attempt to tie together everything that we explored so far.

But in order to preserve some perspective and context, we need to jump back a little.

Some of you might still remember that two weeks ago we talked about the dangers of misleading and about importance of coming back to the source. And where is that source.

We covered accidental and intentional confusion in our faith. We talked about traps that we face on our journey with Christ. That there are times when it is the enemy who tries to mislead and confuse us, but other times we "shoot ourselves into a foot". That happens.

We talked about George Washington. And his death caused by paralagism, unintentional logical mistake. Also, we talked about sophistry - intentional misleading. Hopefully it refreshed your memories a little. And not let's read a couple of verses:

#### Colossians 2:6-7 (NASB95)
> 6 Therefore as you have received Christ Jesus the Lord, so walk in Him, 7 having been firmly rooted and now being built up in Him and established in your faith, just as you were instructed, and overflowing with gratitude.

We can treat this verses as a sort of a "road map" of our christian journey. This is "**the plan**" so to speak.

#### FIRST STEP
First step - is to come to faith. To receive Christ Jesus as the Lord and savior. It's not a destination, as some people think. It's only the entry point. "The door".

#### NEXT STEP
Once we received Christ, we should continue to "walk in Him". Which means to continue to progress on that **path**.

Conveniently verse 7 outlines for us how it typically looks like. It says things like "firmly rooted" - which communicates the idea of **reinforcing the foundation**, growing roots deep into the soil.

If we think about trees and roots, it ensures that tree can withstand harsh winds and severe droughts. Can stand against the challenges of life. It is more hidden, invisible process that happens underground. And unless you'll try to pull the tree out, you not gonna really know how deep the roots are. 

#### SECOND PART
Second part says that we also should be "build up and established" in our faith. Again when you think of trees. That the visible part. With all the branches and leafs. It can represent the visible part of our life.

We not gonna go into details as it is not our topic for today. All this to show that our faith is not static. There should be a progress, development, growth.

This metaphors help us to thing in the right direction. We look at the helpful images, parallels from agriculture, that try to explain things we cannot see, spiritual truths about kingdom of God.

### WEAK TO STRONG
From this verses we see that in the beginning our metaphorical "roots" aren't too deep. Our foundation isn't established just yet. And we need to take active part in order to improve that.

### Only Images
But because we talk using metaphors. Sometimes or maybe even often it's not very clear what exactly we need to do? What does it mean to "**walk in Jesus**" or "**progress in faith**", "**to grow our roots**" and etc. We might think we know the answer. We might say: "It's easy. You need to do X, Y, Z...".

### TRAPS
While we trying to make progress in our faith, trying to figure out how to "root in" and "to establish" and "build up" our faith. We have to watch out of a trap, a pitfall, a danger that we can easily fall into.

...

We can get captivated by **philosophy** and **empty deception**, or like some modern translations put it "**high sounding nonsense.**" Which comes down to following rules/traditions/rituals and mystical thinking/practices. 

Later in verse 16 we see some concrete examples that bible brings up. Things that followers of Christ still fall for. Even today... It's very likely that there is more the one person in this room who actively practices one of those.

Bit later we will go over them. But first let's think about something.

There were a time in the history when worshiping and following God was, or at least expressed though following a set of rules and traditions. One had to belong to specific nation. Had to become one of them, get sercumsiczed, adopt all the law, traditions and rules. Had to become part of their religion.

At modern times people treat faith in Christ as a philosophy. Set of principles, worldview, a helpful framework to look at the world and make it a better place.

But it's not it. Our faith shouldn't be just a philosophy, a set of principles we live by. It's not just "DO's" and "DONT's". None of that.

### WHAT IS FAITH
Everything changed with Jesus. He revealed to us that following God it's not about following certain rules. It's a formula or philosophy. Jesus showed us that **faith in God** - is a living relationships with the Father. Who is not just some abstract idea, the power or cosmic mind. No, we can think about our father in heaven as a concrete person. We made after his image. And therefore we resemble many of his characteristics. Which means we can have **personal relationships** with him. Similar to the relationships we have with one another.

Unfortunately as human beings when it comes to things we cannot see and touch. It becomes hard to think about faith as relationships.

We prefer systems and formulas instead. We see someones experience and we try to turn it into a formula. We try to "crack the code" and get the recipe of success, recipe of healing, formula for anointing.

But once we start chasing formulas and recipes, we lose living and loving relationships.


### HEALING EXAMPLE
Jesus healed countless people during His time. It was one of the things here were known for. But yet in John 5. When Jesus comes to the pool called Bethesda(That's for you all the gamers out there). Bible say there were "*a multitude of those who were sick, blind, limping, or paralyzed.*". There were waiting for the water in the pool to start bubbling. Because then, whoever comes into the water first will get healed.

Jesus was right there. In the mids of all this people. The one who could heal them all. All they need was to simply ask... But they were not interested. Because they already had a formula, a recipe that worked -get into the water faster the anyone else...

#### EXAMPLE
How long do you think your marriage or friendship will last if you will stop having relationships. And start applying formulas to get what you want?

I bet not too long. And maybe you will get what you want a few times. But you will loose the most precious part of it all.

...

We should stop chasing formulas and recipes in our relationships. Especially with God. We should start pursuing the real, living relationships with Him. And then, out of the relationships comes out the blessings, healing, anointing and etc.

That is what so special about Jesus. He modeled this relationships and made them possible. He made a way for us to come to our father. So that we also could have living relationships with Him. 

So our faith is not a framework, mental model or religion. It's not just a philosophy or set of rules and "life hacks". It is a relationship between God and his people.

#### Verse 16-17:
Tell us that old traditions and rules like:

* Regulations about Food and drinks
* Observing specific Festival\New moon
* Observing Sabbath day

This things do not define our relationships with God anymore. Once they were a way to honor God and have a relationships with Him. But everything changed. Bible says that this things are only **the shadow** of what is to come. They are not the thing. [VIDEO ABOUT CRUCIT SHODOW]

...

You see, shadow and the thing itself, not nesseserly the same. **The body**, the real thing. The one that costs that shadow **is Christ**. In other words, all those rituals and rules only point to Christ. When Jesus came He revealed to us what the Kingdom of God is really about. What relationships with God really are.

He called us His friends. He showed us the Father. We do not need to look at the shadow anymore. We already have a real thing.

That's why Bible warns us in verse 18:

#### Verse 18:
> *Don’t let anyone to keep defrauding you of your price.*

Paul borrows this image from sports. He portrays a bad judge who makes a faulty or biased call, which result in stripping away the reward that rightfully belongs to an athlete.

We shouldn't let anyone to rob us off our reward, our privilege of having living relationships with God. 

Similar to  verse 8, verse 18 says that we should be aware of clever rhetoric's that masks a false wisdom:

*By insisting on false humility, angelic worship, and going about visions he had seen.* Ultimately people claim  superior spirituality. Which however simply masking pride taken in fleshly mind.

Living relationship getting substituted with following rules and mistical\magical thinking.

Such people claim to be spiritual entrepreneurs. Claim to be different, above average, above mere mortals, "regular" Christians. They have scores of  revelations and visions "from God" that avalible to them only. Or to a very limited number of selected people...

#### Verse 19:
But bible says that, they are actually "*detached from the head*". Detached from Christ.

Typically they are not a part of any specific local church. They desire to be leaders, teachers and etc.

But only as a part of the body you can stay alive. Because it is the body that getting nourished. Being held **together** by joints and ligaments.

The connections we have with one another are extremely important. Being part of the body is vital.

Because God gives growth to a body as a unit. 

### Verse 20-23:
And in the verses 20-23 Paul explains that if we truly with Christ then there is no point in trying to submit yourselves to those old ways. There is absolutely no reason to willingly give up living relationships with God, and exchange them to some traditions. 

There is no need to add those "additional" things. If you wasn't born as a jew and adopted those traditions as you became Christian. It only shows a lack of faith Christ. They are of no benefit for you.

If you truly believe in Christ. If you truly died with him to this world. Why are you still act as a part of this world? Why are you still practice rituals that only point to Christ?

None of them are able to achieve the goal. They not gonna give you relationships. They not gonna set you free from sin. 

### Verse 23:
And all these things are surely appear like wisdom. In self imposed religious practices, humility and severity toward the body/asceticism. But actually useless in what they claim to be achieving. 

Many of those rituals, recopies and formulas make sense as a part of human made religion. They truly look and sound like wisdom. But, they are hallow, they are empty inside. They have nothing to do with what God wants for us.

### Conclusion:
God wants to have a real relationships with me and you. It's not a metaphor. It is the truth. And Jesus is the only way that makes it possible.

Our faith is about relationships with God and with one another. We need to stop listening to that clever sounding nonsense and persuasive talks. And start concentrating on knowing God and hearing his voice.

If you have never done it. Or haven't experienced this for a while. Find a quite place, where you can be alone and not disturbed for a while. Where your mind will be free from all the distractions. And genuinely, sincerely talk to your Father. 

You can say: "My father who is in heaven. I want to hear from you. Please, speak to me. Teach me how to hear and recognize your voice. I ask in the name of my Lord Jesus Christ. Amen..."

And watch what happens... Spent time listening. You might hear something right there. You might not. And it's OK. If you are genuinely seeking to know God and hear from Him, and have relationships with Him. Things will start changing...
