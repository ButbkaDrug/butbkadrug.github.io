Let's read copule verse from the beginning of the chapter:

#### Colossians 2:6-7 (NASB95)
> 6 Therefore as you have received Christ Jesus the Lord, so walk in Him, 7 having been firmly rooted and now being built up in Him and established in your faith, just as you were instructed, and overflowing with gratitude.

We can treat this two verses as a "roadmap" of our christian journey. This is "the plan" so to speak. First you step is to come to faith - to receive Christ Jesus as the Lord and savior. It's not our destination. It's only the entry point. 

Once we received Christ, we should "walk in Him". Which means to continue our progress in that direction. And verse 7 describes how it should look like. And it says things like "firmly rooted" - which speaks the idea of reinforcing the foundation, but also it says "to build up and establish" - to develop further.

All this to show that our faith is not static. There should be some development happening. Remember it's all only images that try to explain to us things we cannot see, thought the every day examples.

### WEAK TO STRONG
From this verses we see that in the beginning our "roots" aren't too deep. Our foundation isn't established just yet. And we need to take active part to change that.

### Only Images
But because it is only images. Sometimes or maybe even often it's not very clear what to do? What does it mean to "walk in Jesus" or "progress in faith". That's when we face a next challenge described in verse 8.

*(And by the way. If you think it's not about you, because you've been a Christen for X number of year. Or because you finished some bible collages\seminary\whatever. Don't be so sure. It's very likely that you actually subjected to one of those.)*

With that said let's read the verse 8:

#### Colossians 2:8 (NASB95)
> See to it that no one takes you captive through philosophy and empty deception, according to the tradition of men, according to the elementary principles of the world, rather than according to Christ.

### Traps
While we making progress in our faith, trying to figure out how to "root" and "establish" and "build up". There is a danger to get captivated by **philosophy** and **empty deception**, or like some modern translation puts it "high sounding nonsense." Which comes down to following rules/traditions/rituals and mystical thinking and practices. 
But it's not! Not at all. Our faith shouldn't be a philosophy we live by. Or a set of rules and principles. A framework we use to think about this world. It's not just "DO's" and "DONT's". None of that.

### What is faith
Jesus revealed to us that following God it's not just following certain rules. It's a formula or philosophy. Jesus showed us that faith in God is a living relationships with the Father.

As people we love systems and formulas. We see someones experience and we try to turn it into a formula. We try to "crack the code" and get the formula of success, formula for healing, formula for anoyinting. But once we start chasing formulas, we lose living and loving relationships.

Jesus healed countless people during His time. It was one of the things here were known for. But yet in John 5. When Jesus comes to the pool called Bethesda. Bible say there were "*a multitude of those who were sick, blind, limping, or paralyzed.*". There were waiting for the water in the pool to start bubbling. Because then, whoever comes into the water first will get healed.

They had Jesus in their mids. The one who could heal them all. But they were not interested. Because they already had a formula, proven way to get the healing. All you need is to get into the water faster the anyone else.

Stop chasing formulas. And start pursuing the relationships. Out of relationships comes out blessings, healing, annoyinting and etc.

And that is what so special about Jesus. He made this relationships possible. By the death on the cross. As we read last week. And also he modeled this relationships for us.

Let's go though the verses and see what they say:

#### Verse 9-15(we read them last week)
They explain to us that Jesus already won the battle against this world. Against spiritual powers and principalities. And when we getting baptised we die to their power as well and raise again by faith in power of God.

So, Principalities and spiritual powers have no more authority over us. Jesus took care of that all by himself.

#### Verse 16-17:
Tell us that old traditions and rules like:

* Regulations about Food and drinks
* Observing specific Festival\New moon
* Observing Sabbath day

This things do not define our relationships with God anymore. Once they were a way to honor God. And in the only way to have a relationships with Him was to become a part of his people and follow this laws and rituals.

But everything changed. Bible says that this things are only **the shadow** of what is to come. They are not the thing. [VIDEO ABOUT CRUCIT SHODOW]

But **the body**, the real thing. The one that costs that shadow **is Christ**. In other words, all those retuals and rules only point to Christ. When Jesus came He revealed to us what the Kingdom of God is really about. What relationships with God really are.

He called us His friends. He showed us the Father. We do not need to look at the shadow anymore. We already have a real thing.

And that's why Bible says in the verse 18:

#### Verse 18:
> *Don’t let anyone to keep defrauding you of your price.*

Again apostle borrows the image from sports. He portrays a bad judge who makes a faulty or biased call, which result in stripping away the reward that rightfully belongs to an athlete.

We shouldn't let anyone to rob us off our reward, our privilege of having living relationships with God. 

Like a verse 8, the one we started with verse 18 says that it's done by clever rhetoric's, though a false wisdom.

*By insisting on false humility, angelic worship, and going about visions he had seen.* Ultimately people claim  superior spirituality. Which however simply masking pride taken in fleshly mind.

Living relationship getting substituted with following rules and mistical\magical thinking.

#### Verse 19:
Such people claim to be spiritual anterpronuers. Claim to be different, above average, mere mortals, regular Christians. They operate on revelations and visions "from God". But actually bible says they are "*detached from the head*". Detached from Christ.

Typically they are not a part of any specific local church. They desire to be leaders, teachers and etc.

But only as a part of the body you can stay alive. Because it is the body that getting nourished. Being held **together** by joints and ligaments. Through whom comes the growth.

### Verse 20-23:
And in the verses 20-23 Paul explains that if we truly with Christ then there is no point to try to submit yourselves to those old ways. There is absolutely no reason to willingly give up living relationships with God, and exchange them to some traditions. 

If you truly believe in Christ. If you truly died with him to this world, and consider yourself part of his kingdom. Why are you still act as a part of this world? Why are you still practice rituals that only point to Christ? Rituals like.

None of them are able to achieve the goal. They not gonna give you relationships. They not gonna set you free from sin. 

### Verse 23:
And all these things are surely appear like wisdom. In self imposed religious practices, humility and severity toward the body/asceticism. But actually useless in what they claim to be achieving. 


They make sense as a part of human made religion. But they have nothing to do with what God wants for us.
